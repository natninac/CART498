### Design Pillars

Fun & Passion

I then based the design pillars on the needs of this user. In my research, musicians were described as passionate about music, and often embedded themselves into the musical scene of 
their city. They would work jobs as sound technicians, music journalists, etc. to support their music career before it takes off. This made me decide on fun being a design pillar of the interface.

Social

I found out that networking is a big differentiator in what makes a musician successful, so there must be a social aspect to the app to assist users in their pursuits.


Discovery

The discovery aspect is where spectators and artists collide. This should allow interested audience members to find music that corresponds to what they like, and it should allow artists
to be easily discovered and to network/collaborate with their peers.
